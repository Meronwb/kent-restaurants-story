Exploring Food Culture in Kent, Washington

A GeoNarrative of restaurants, cuisine, and community

This project explores the restaurant landscape in Kent, Washington through maps, images, and text.

Created by Meron Birhanu | University of Washington

Scene 1: Food and Place in Kent

Kent is home to a growing and diverse food scene.

Scene 2: Diversity of Cuisine

Restaurants in Kent represent a range of cuisines and food traditions.

Scene 3: Restaurant Clusters and Access

Restaurant locations are not evenly distributed throughout Kent.

Scene 4: Featured Places

A few restaurants stand out because of their atmosphere and personal meaning.

Project Information

GEOG 458 Lab 07 | Map-based Storytelling Project

Data sources: Restaurant locations collected from online map sources and converted into GeoJSON for mapping.
