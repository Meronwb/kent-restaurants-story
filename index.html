Exploring Food Culture in Kent, Washington

A GeoNarrative of restaurants, cuisine, and community

This project explores the restaurant landscape in Kent, Washington through maps, images, and text. It highlights cuisine diversity, restaurant clustering, and the relationship between food and place.

Created by Meron Birhanu | University of Washington

STEP 1: Food and Place in Kent

Kent is home to a growing and diverse food scene. Restaurants across the city provide not only meals, but also spaces where culture, community, and everyday life come together.

STEP 2: Diversity of Cuisine

Restaurants in Kent represent a range of cuisines and food traditions. Mapping these locations helps show how the city’s food landscape reflects cultural diversity.

STEP 3: Restaurant Clusters and Access

Restaurant locations are not evenly distributed throughout Kent. Many are found near major roads, shopping centers, and commercial corridors.

STEP 4: Featured Places

A few restaurants stand out because of their atmosphere, cuisine, or personal meaning. These featured places add a more personal layer to the map story.
