Exploring Food Culture in Kent, Washington

A GeoNarrative of restaurants, cuisine, and community

This project explores the restaurant landscape in Kent, Washington through an online geo-narrative. By combining maps, images, and text, the project shows how restaurants reflect cultural diversity, local identity, and patterns of access across the city.

Created by Meron Birhanu | University of Washington

Scene 1: Food and Place in Kent

Kent is home to a growing and diverse food scene. Restaurants across the city provide not only meals, but also spaces where culture, community, and everyday life come together. This story begins with an overview of Kent and the restaurant locations included in this project.

Scene 2: Diversity of Cuisine

Restaurants in Kent represent a range of cuisines and food traditions. Mapping these locations helps show how the city’s food landscape reflects cultural diversity and the presence of different communities.

Scene 3: Restaurant Clusters and Access

Restaurant locations are not evenly distributed throughout Kent. Many are found near major roads, shopping centers, and commercial corridors. These patterns suggest that development and transportation play an important role in shaping where food access is concentrated.

Scene 4: Featured Places

A few restaurants stand out because of their atmosphere, cuisine, or personal importance. These featured places add a more personal perspective to the map and help connect geography with lived experience.
